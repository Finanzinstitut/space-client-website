On the desktop

The launcher

Create instances, find mods, switch accounts — without opening a browser.

  • Multiple accounts through Microsoft sign-in, switched without restarting.
  • Modrinth built in — search, install, dependencies come along.
  • Import modpacks from .mrpack files.
  • Any Java version and any mod loader, set per instance.
  • Live console for every running instance, straight from the list.
  • Preinstall Cosmetica when creating an instance, one checkbox.

Music

Now Playing

Space Client reads the track from your Spotify or Amazon Music desktop app and shows it on the HUD, with playback controls while chat is open.

  • On your HUD — title, artist and the app it came from.
  • Over your name — other Space Client players see what you are listening to above your nametag. Off by default.
  • Lyrics over your name — the current line instead of the title, for players who turn it on.

Lyrics are covered by copyright, and whether you may display them depends on the song and where you are. The setting is off by default and using it is your own responsibility.

Looks

Cosmetics through Cosmetica

Capes, wings, bandanas and horns come from Cosmetica, reachable straight from the Space Client menu. They live on your Cosmetica account, so they follow you onto any server.
